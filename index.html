// Philosophy

How I Approach Security Engineering

Security isn't an afterthought checklist or noisy alert queue; it is an active discipline of architecture, behavioral telemetry, and threat comprehension.

Fail-Closed by Design

I believe tools must refuse to proceed when state cannot be explicitly trusted. In VYRA, STS caller account verification aborts execution before inspecting any AWS resource, ensuring zero inadvertent cross-account leakage.

Telemetry Over Noise

Alert fatigue destroys SOC effectiveness. In my Wazuh SOC Homelab, I pair Windows Security logs (4624, 4625, 4728) with a 6-rule correlation engine that tests for multi-target password spraying and MFA fatigue instead of naive one-off thresholds.

Adversary-Centric Curiosity

Effective defense requires knowing how adversaries operate. My research into attacks like Midnight Blizzard and Change Healthcare directly shapes my policy gates and identity federation checks.

Nation-State APT 2024

Microsoft Midnight Blizzard

Password Spraying & OAuth Token Abuse

Analysis of Nobelium's campaign against corporate email systems. The adversary sprayed passwords against a non-MFA legacy test tenant, pivoted to grant high-privilege permissions to an OAuth app, and accessed leadership mailboxes.

  • ▸ Root Cause: Legacy unmonitored test tenant without Conditional Access or MFA.
  • ▸ Detection Rule: Flag sudden Exchange MailItemsAccessed spikes from multi-tenant enterprise apps.
  • ▸ Remediation: Automated decommissioning of inactive test tenants and FIDO2 enforcement.
ALPHV / BlackCat 2024

Change Healthcare Ransomware

Single-Factor Remote Portal Credential Theft

Deep-dive into the incident response, containment priorities, and third-party supplier dependencies of the largest healthcare clearinghouse disruption in US history.

  • ▸ Initial Access: Compromised credentials on an external Citrix portal lacking multi-factor authentication.
  • ▸ Lateral Movement: Rapid exfiltration of 6TB+ of sensitive healthcare data followed by ransomware encryption.
  • ▸ Takeaway: Zero single-factor access policies, third-party dependency mapping, and immutable backup isolation.
DarkSide Ransomware 2021

Colonial Pipeline Ransomware

Legacy VPN Credential Leak & OT Shutdown

Investigation into the attack timeline, access control weaknesses, network segmentation failures, and containment choices that caused nationwide fuel pipeline shutdown.

  • ▸ Entry Point: Reused password found on a dark-web dump used against an unmanaged legacy VPN account.
  • ▸ Containment Impact: OT pipeline was shut down proactively due to lack of visibility into billing systems.
  • ▸ Lesson: Stringent air-gapping between IT/OT domains and automated stale account disabling.
